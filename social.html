Social Transformation Programs

At LeadAfrique International, we believe true leadership extends beyond the individual—it transforms societies. Our Social Transformation Programs are designed to empower communities, strengthen institutions, and create systems that foster inclusive growth. These initiatives target governance, corporate excellence, and family empowerment, ensuring holistic transformation from the household to the nation.

Empowering Women in Politics

Overview

The Empowering Women in Politics (EWIP) program; funded by the Centre Party International Foundation and implemented in Ghana by LeadAfrique International is dedicated to increasing the capacity and participation of women in politics. Recognizing the barriers women face in political spaces, EWIP equips aspiring and current female leaders with the knowledge, skills, and networks they need to thrive and influence change at all levels of leadership.

Our Focus

EWIP goes beyond training—it creates a safe and empowering space for women to learn, connect, and lead. The program supports women across the political spectrum, helping them to:

  • Build confidence and resilience for political leadership.
  • Acquire practical campaign and governance skills.
  • Network with mentors, role models, and peers.
  • Advocate for inclusive and gender-sensitive policies.

Core Components

  • Leadership Training: Intensive workshops and seminars on campaign management, communication, negotiation, and policy engagement.
  • Mentorship: Pairing women with seasoned political leaders and professionals for guidance and real-world insights.
  • Networking Platforms: Creating spaces for cross-party dialogue, alliances, and collaboration among women in politics.
  • Advocacy & Visibility: Raising the profile of women leaders and advocating for systems that make political spaces more inclusive.

Impact So Far

Since its launch, EWIP has:

  • Trained and supported hundreds of women from diverse political and professional backgrounds.
  • Contributed to stronger female representation in local assemblies, political parties, and advocacy groups.
  • Built a supportive community of women leaders who champion inclusivity and transformation in governance.

The EWIP Network

EWIP is not just a program—it is a growing movement of women who believe in using political power for positive social transformation. Alumni continue to engage in mentorship, community projects, and advocacy campaigns, creating ripple effects of change across Ghana and beyond.

  • Sponsor training sessions or mentorship activities.
  • Provide platforms for EWIP alumnae to showcase their leadership.
  • Collaborate on advocacy initiatives for gender equality in governance.

Ghana Youth Leadership Academy (GYLA)

About GYLA

The Ghana Youth Leadership Academy (GYLA), founded in 2022 by LeadAfrique International, is a flagship leadership programme that equips young people with the competence, confidence, and civic mindset needed to transform communities and shape Ghana’s future. We believe in the power of youth to influence governance, drive social change, and strengthen democracy.

Our Approach

GYLA combines learning, practice, and mentorship to nurture transformative leaders:

  • Residential Leadership Camps – immersive training with experts, simulations, and peer learning.
  • Bi-Weekly Online Sessions – interactive discussions and book clubs on leadership and personal growth.
  • Mentorship Programme – one-on-one guidance from leaders in public, private, and civic sectors.
  • Community Advocacy Projects – youth-led initiatives tackling real issues in schools and communities.
  • Alumni Engagement – the annual Black Star Summit and ongoing networks keep the movement alive.

Impact (2022–2025)

  • 180 fellows trained through camps and online sessions.
  • 42 advocacy projects implemented across Ghana.
  • 150+ duty bearers engaged, including MPs, assemblies, and ministries.
  • 4,500+ direct beneficiaries and 6.3 Million of indirect beneficiaries reached.

Fellows achieved remarkable milestones:

  • Advocated for a 28% increase in the National Service allowance.
  • Brought national attention to all uncompleted projects.

Alumni & Sustainability

The journey doesn’t end after graduation. Through the Black Star Summit, GYLA alumni reconnect annually to share ideas, network, and renew their commitment to leadership. With over 250 young leaders convened in 2025, the Summit ensures that GYLA’s impact grows stronger every year.

The Black Star Summit

The Black Star Summit is GYLA’s flagship alumni conference — a space where young leaders reconnect, reflect, and re-energize their commitment to transforming Ghana. Launched in May 2025, the Summit brings together alumni of GYLA alongside participants from other leadership and civic training programmes. It is designed as an annual platform to sustain the spirit of the Academy by providing continuous learning, networking, and advocacy opportunities.

What Happens at the Summit?

  • Dialogues & Workshops – Interactive sessions on governance, social change, entrepreneurship, and youth development.
  • Policy Engagement – Recommendations and ideas generated by fellows are compiled into a rapporteur’s report, submitted to the Ministry of Youth Development and Empowerment and other stakeholders.
  • Networking & Collaboration – Fellows connect with peers, mentors, and institutional partners to scale their initiatives and create collective impact.
  • Celebration of Impact – Alumni success stories are showcased, inspiring the next wave of youth leaders.

2025 Highlights

  • Convened 250+ young leaders from across Ghana.
  • Hosted cross-sector conversations between youth, civil society, and policymakers.
  • Produced a policy report capturing the voices and recommendations of Ghana’s young leaders.
  • Sparked alumni-led collaborations that will continue beyond the event.

Looking Ahead

The Black Star Summit will be held every year, serving as the anchor for GYLA’s alumni network. By institutionalizing this annual gathering, LeadAfrique ensures that GYLA is not just a 4-year programme, but a lifelong movement of youth leaders shaping Ghana’s democratic and development agenda. The Black Star Summit is more than a conference. It is a movement of young leaders committed to shining as brightly as Ghana’s Black Star.

Parent Support and Family Enrichment

We recognize that families are the bedrock of society. Our programmes equip parents and families with tools to raise principled, resilient, and purpose-driven children, while strengthening family bonds.

Mothers Lifting Mothers (MLM)

Overview

Mothers carry unique responsibilities in shaping the next generation. MLM creates a circle of encouragement, mentorship, and practical guidance where mothers support each other to thrive in parenting, career, and personal life.

Focus

  • Emotional resilience and self-care for mothers
  • Practical parenting strategies
  • Peer mentoring and shared growth

Impact

MLM has helped mothers build strong support systems, reduce burnout, and rediscover joy in motherhood.

Fathers Lifting Fathers (FLF)

Overview

Fatherhood is central to raising grounded children, yet many fathers feel isolated in their parenting journey. FLF brings fathers together to share wisdom, address challenges, and strengthen their role as protectors, providers, and mentors.

Focus

  • Fatherhood and leadership in the home
  • Work-life balance and financial responsibility
  • Creating intentional bonds with children

Impact

FLF has empowered fathers to become more present, engaged, and impactful in their families and communities.

Overview

Parents’ Nest is a collective support hub where both mothers and fathers come together as a community of learners and encouragers. It provides a holistic environment for parents to grow as individuals, partners, and caregivers.

Focus

  • Family strengthening and joint parenting approaches
  • Shared parenting experiences and peer learning
  • Building homes that nurture confidence and values in children

Impact

Parents’ Nest has cultivated communities of parents who collaborate, share resources, and model healthy family life for their children.

Xplore

Overview

Xplore provides local and international tours, expeditions, and exchange programs that strengthen families and broaden horizons. It is designed to help children and families bond, appreciate diversity, and cultivate independence.

Impact

Participants return with expanded worldviews, stronger family ties, and greater confidence to thrive in diverse environments.

Awofuo Conference

Overview

The Awofuo Conference is LeadAfrique International’s premier annual gathering dedicated to parents. Rooted in the belief that “it takes a village to raise a child,” the conference equips parents with the knowledge, tools, and networks they need to raise principled, resilient, and purpose-driven children in today’s rapidly changing world.

Why Awofuo?

The word “Awofuo” reflects the sacred role of parents in shaping the next generation. This conference provides a safe space for mothers, fathers, and guardians to reflect, learn, and share strategies that strengthen families and, by extension, society.

Focus Areas

The conference brings together experts, facilitators, and parents to engage on various pressing themes that affect the modern parent.

Impact

The Awofuo Conference is designed to reach 500 parents annually, empowering them with knowledge and strategies to transform their families. By strengthening parents, we indirectly shape children who are confident, principled, and ready to lead Africa into the future.
