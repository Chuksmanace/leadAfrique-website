Empowering individuals to reach their full potential.

Teacher Transformation Program (TTP)

Overview

Teachers are more than instructors—they are role models, mentors, and influencers. The TTP equips teachers to fulfill this vital role by focusing on their personal mastery, professional growth, and financial empowerment.

Focus

  • Personal development and self-mastery
  • Pedagogical skills and professional excellence
  • Financial literacy and personal finance management

Impact

Over 2,200 teachers across 9 countries have been trained, shifting hearts, minds, and teaching practices to inspire students toward their full potential.

Learning to Learn (L2L)

Overview

Many students struggle, not because of ability, but because they lack effective study techniques. Learning-to-Learn equips them with strategies to study smarter, understand better, and retain knowledge.

Focus

  • Identifying learning styles
  • Effective study habits
  • Critical thinking and knowledge retention

Impact

Thousands of students have improved their academic performance and built lifelong learning skills through this program.

Leadership Farm

Overview

Our flagship in-school leadership development programme. Through weekly sessions, young people are guided to discover their potential, build character, and cultivate habits that empower them to lead themselves and others.

Focus

  • Self-awareness and self-leadership
  • Purpose discovery and values-based living
  • Building resilience, responsibility, and confidence

Impact

Since 2014, over 50,000 students across Ghana, Liberia, Uganda, and Kenya have participated, transforming classrooms into spaces of inspiration and leadership growth.

Leadership Farm Summer Camp

Overview

A one-week transformative experience that brings together young people (ages 6–20) for leadership, personal development, and global exposure.

Focus

  • Leadership and soft skills training
  • Purpose discovery and goal-setting
  • Exposure to cultural diversity and global competencies

Impact

Hundreds of young leaders have graduated with improved confidence, self-esteem, and leadership ability.

Goal Setting for Young People

Overview

This programme empowers young people to think long-term, set meaningful goals, and build daily habits that align with their values and aspirations.

Focus

  • Life planning and visioning
  • Daily discipline and productivity skills
  • Cultivating resilience to overcome challenges

Impact

The program has helped young people develop clarity of thought and practical roadmaps to live fulfilled lives.

CR8TIFF Business Plan Competition

Overview

A national competition that nurtures problem-solving and critical thinking among high school students, supporting them to design practical solutions to pressing social problems.

Focus

  • Entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Problem-solving and creativity
  • Business plan development and pitching

Impact

The competition has inspired dozens of groundbreaking ideas and provided a platform to transform concepts into actionable projects.

Teens Hangout

Overview

Provides a safe space for teenagers to explore unique challenges like peer pressure, identity struggles, and transitions, and learn to make responsible decisions.

Focus

  • Navigating friendships, school, and family
  • Building healthy self-esteem and resilience
  • Preparing for transitions to boarding school and higher education

Impact

Participants leave empowered with tools to face challenges with confidence, clarity, and purpose.

National School Leavers Fair

Overview

A 3-day program supporting students transitioning from junior and senior high school into their next stage of life.

Focus

  • WorkReady skills development
  • Exposure to diverse career and learning opportunities
  • Guidance for tertiary education and entrepreneurship

Impact

The Fair equips school leavers with practical tools to embrace change, pursue their passions, and thrive.

Career Guidance

Overview

Helps young people align their strengths, values, and aspirations to navigate evolving career paths in a rapidly changing world.

Focus

  • Self-discovery and career planning
  • Understanding future skills and opportunities
  • Building roadmaps for career success

Impact

This programme has empowered students to make informed, value-driven decisions about their future careers.

Prefects & Tertiary Education Leadership Programme

Overview

Equips student leaders, who are often elected without adequate preparation, with the leadership and management skills needed to succeed.

Focus

  • Leadership principles and ethics
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Conflict management and problem-solving

Impact

Graduates serve as more effective leaders in their schools and later as impactful leaders in society.

Leadership for Educationists Programme (LEAP)

Overview

Builds the leadership and managerial skills of school heads and principals, enabling them to lead with vision, strategy, and empathy.

Focus

  • Leadership and soft skills for school heads
  • Strategic thinking and organizational growth
  • People management and staff motivation

Impact

Dozens of principals have gained new leadership capacity, resulting in stronger schools and better learning outcomes.

Overview
